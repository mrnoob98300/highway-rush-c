HIGHWAY RUSH 3D — REALISTIC VISUAL UPDATE

HOW TO PLAY
1. Extract the entire ZIP into a folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Select JOURNEY or ENDLESS and press START.
No server, npm install, CDN, or model download is required to play.
The game requires WebGL 2 with browser hardware acceleration enabled.

CONTROLS
Hold Left/Right or A/D: gradual steering.
Touch: use the left/right buttons or drag on the game.
Space/Up or NITRO: boost after the blue nitro meter fills.
P or pause button: pause/resume. Speaker button: mute/unmute.

FILES
index.html, style.css, game.js — game UI and gameplay.
world3d.js — bundled WebGL renderer (Three.js + GLTFLoader).
assets.js — embedded models, avoids local-file fetch/CORS issues.
assets/ — original GLBs plus earlier lightweight runtime copies. New foliage,
body surface refinements and textures are generated by source/world.js at startup.
fonts/ — included fonts. licenses/ — third-party licenses.
source/world.js — editable renderer source.

OPTIONAL RENDERER REBUILD (only needed after editing source/world.js)

PERFORMANCE UPDATE
- Default Auto starts in Balanced and drops to Smooth on sustained slow frames.
- Graphics menu offers Auto, High, Balanced and Smooth; the choice is saved locally.
- Resolution and pixel count are capped; High enables realtime shadows.
- Balanced/Smooth keep car contact shading without a full shadow render pass.
- Offscreen trees are culled, distant foliage uses lower detail, and equivalent materials are merged.
- Traffic keeps detailed bodywork with simpler round wheels and merged meshes.
- Pickups/templates are cached and transient objects are reused.
- Fixed-step physics keeps steering consistent; racing render rate is capped at 60 FPS.
- Garage renders at up to 30 FPS; paused view up to 10 FPS. Hidden tabs pause racing.
- HUD updates are throttled; costly live backdrop blur is removed.
- Four tree species, curved track, coins, nitro, health, garage and save data are retained.
